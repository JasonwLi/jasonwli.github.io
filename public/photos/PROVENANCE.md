# Photo provenance

Every image under `public/photos/` is an original photograph taken by Jason Li, exported from his own Apple Photos library by the pipeline in `scripts/photo-pipeline/` (people-filtered, resized to 1600 px plus an 800 px medium variant, EXIF and GPS stripped). None are generated, stock, or third-party images. Origin is recorded here once rather than inside each JPEG so the committed photo binaries are never rewritten.
